# Product Strategy & Monetization

## 🎯 Target Audience
The SVG Grafik Generator aims at two primary segments:
1. **Developers & Designers**: Need quick, customizable icons for web projects without high costs.
2. **Community Platforms (e.g., Bikepark Okarben)**: Require scalable, personalizable avatar systems for users.

## 💰 Monetization Model (Proposed)

### Free Tier
- Access to 250+ standard icons.
- Basic SVG customization.
- 5 starter rider presets.

### Premium Tier ($2.99 / Month)
- Access to exclusive Icon Packs.
- Advanced Rider Avatar components (Level 20+).
- Custom Branding (removed "Made with ❤️" tag).
- Batch Download features.

### Pro / Enterprise Tier
- AI-powered custom icon generation.
- High-resolution PNG/WebP exports.
- API Access for external apps (e.g., RP-Service Integration).

## 🚀 Future Business Goals
1. **White-Labeling**: Allow other bikeparks or communities to host their own themed avatar generators.
2. **AI Marketplace**: A space where users can trade or sell their prompt-generated SVG designs.
3. **Integration**: Direct plugins for Figma or Canva.
